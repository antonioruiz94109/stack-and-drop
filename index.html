What does drop mean?

Heel-to-toe drop is the difference between the sole’s height beneath your heel and forefoot. A 30 mm heel and 22 mm forefoot give an 8 mm drop.

Drop alone doesn’t tell you whether a shoe suits walking, daily running, or racing. Use Intended use for that, and use drop to narrow a preference you already know.
